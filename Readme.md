## 🌙 About

This repository is a **living collection of wallpapers** inspired by:

- Anime moods, frames, and emotions  
- Dark, minimal, and soft aesthetics  
- Calm, lo-fi, late-night energy  
- Visuals that don't scream — they *whisper*

The focus is **intentional curation**, not random accumulation.  
Small today. Refined over time. Always aesthetic-first.

## 📁 Repository Structure

### 🧑🏻‍🎤 Anime

Anime wallpapers that emphasize **emotion, atmosphere, and composition** over visual noise.

```text
anime/
 ┣ 🖥️ desktop        – wide-format anime wallpapers
 ┣ 💕 waifus         – favorite characters and portraits
 ┗ 🗂️ misc           – uncategorized anime aesthetics
```

###  📺 LiveWallpapers
```text
LiveWalls/
 ┣ 💠 dynamic and "alive" lively wallpapers
```
###  🚗 Vehicles
```text
Cars/Supercars/Hybrids
 ┣ 💠 Retro, electric, sports, F1, F2
```

### 🌑 OneDark

A dark-first, minimal wallpaper collection inspired by technology, space, architecture, and calm visual storytelling.

```text
onedark/
 ┣ 🎮 gaming-botw          – dark gaming aesthetics
 ┣ 🐧 linux-os-logos       – OS & distro-inspired visuals
 ┣ 🧩 minimal-abstract     – clean, low-noise compositions
 ┣ 🌲 nature-landscapes    – moody natural scenes
 ┣ 💻 programs-apps        – developer & software aesthetics
 ┣ 🎬 scifi-movies         – cinematic & futuristic tones
 ┣ 🌌 space-cosmic         – stars, voids, and silence
 ┣ 📼 tech-retro           – retro-futurism & old-tech vibes
 ┣ 🚆 transportation       – machines in motion
 ┣ 🏙️ urban-architecture  – city geometry & structures
 ┣ 🪟 window-managers     – minimal desktop aesthetics
 ┣ so on.........
 ┗ 📦 uncategorized       – temporary holding space
```

**Design philosophy:**  
Dark backgrounds · soft contrast · minimal clutter  
Every wallpaper should feel intentional and timeless.

## 🖼️ Usage

1. Browse a folder that matches your mood
2. Open a wallpaper you like
3. Download it in full resolution
4. Set it as your wallpaper
5. Let the screen breathe ✨

Suitable for desktop, laptop, and mobile.

- No watermarks
- No cluttered compositions
- Anime wallpapers must feel aesthetic, not chaotic

**Quality always beats quantity.**
